## Latar joglo — design/assets/joglo-forest.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic background artwork for an Indonesian wedding website, NOT a UI mockup. Create a richly detailed, elegant cinematic 3D-rendered Javanese teak joglo entrance from Surakarta/Solo inside a lush deep emerald tropical forest. Camera perfectly centered at human height, standing just outside an open joglo doorway looking through into a hazy forest with warm morning light. Traditional authentic high multi-tier timber joglo roof, two tall carved warm brown teak posts at left and right, subtle Solo kawung carving, mossy stones, ferns, dark burgundy fabric accent small at extreme edges. The central 55% area is open, relatively dark green and uncluttered for later white website text; perspective leads into the opening. Architectural entrance fills portrait frame top and edges, not tiny in distance. Clear layered depth: dark foreground trees sides, midground joglo, distant atmospheric forest. Photorealistic materials and believable 3D game environment lighting, refined atmospheric forest wedding mood. Portrait aspect ratio 2:3, high detail. Palette emerald forest green, warm teak brown, warm white light, restrained burgundy. No people, no faces, no human silhouettes, no ceremony objects, no deities, no Balinese gate or temple, no Japanese torii, no text, no monogram, no letters, no UI, no watermark. This is a reusable scenic architectural asset, not the real event venue.

## Foreground transparan — design/assets/forest-foreground.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: TRANSPARENT foreground tree cutout for a multi-layer parallax wedding website. Create one tall tropical forest edge consisting of a richly textured mature tree trunk leaning inward very slightly, attached large fern fronds, hanging vines, moss and glossy deep emerald leaves. Cinematic photorealistic 3D render with soft warm morning rim lighting from upper right. Palette deep green #183D32, fern green #526B46, warm teak brown #765640, subtle light edges. Composition portrait 2:3: trunk mostly in LEFT third, branches and leafy crown framing upper left, lush ferns framing lower left; right two-thirds mostly TRANSPARENT open air, individual leaves and fronds reaching softly into the center, recognizable sculptural tree silhouette. This single edge will be mirrored in code for the right side and repeated as a depth layer in each section. Real alpha transparency across all empty space and holes between leaves. Isolated botanical foreground only: no floor plane, no scenery, no sky, no backdrop, no background color, no solid shadow behind subject, no text, no icons, no frames, no human, no faces, no watermarks. Do not create a rectangle of forest; the silhouette must be visibly cut out with transparent surroundings.

PNG foreground telah diperiksa: RGBA, alpha 0–254. Tidak menggunakan potret manusia. Lapisan depan dan latar bergerak terpisah dalam kode; preferensi reduced motion menonaktifkan gerak.

### Prompt paper amplop — referensi undangan fisik

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: floral burgundy paper texture panel to be applied to CSS 3D envelope flaps, NOT a finished UI and NOT a mockup. Input image is a STYLE REFERENCE of the user's physical wedding invitation: follow its burgundy-red tone, tone-on-tone wooded landscape illustration, detailed burgundy flowers and ivory chrysanthemums/dahlia roses at lower side corners. Create a clean portrait 2:3 full-bleed burgundy paper panel. Elegant classical Javanese fine stationery: deep burgundy #711922 red wine surface, soft embossed tree silhouettes tone-on-tone in the background, restrained warm brown and muted antique bronze carved ornament line at edges. Rich bouquets of burgundy flowers and ivory white flowers framing lower left and lower right, trailing upward along the outer edges; leave the central 65 percent and the upper middle mostly clean dark burgundy for names and guest text to be overlaid in HTML. Realistic printed paper texture with subtle depth, exquisite flat botanical illustration in the style of the physical reference, not photorealistic jungle trees in front. NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO NAMES, NO LETTERS, no typography, no date, no monogram, no people, no faces, no wax seal, no watermark, no UI, no screen borders. Do not copy the screenshot, editor interface, two-panel layout or words. One single clean reusable burgundy floral paper artwork, no folded envelope rendering because flaps will be rendered in code.

`public/assets/joglo-forest.webp`, `public/assets/forest-foreground.webp`, dan `public/assets/envelope-botanical.webp` dipakai oleh frontend. Sumber PNG disimpan di `design/assets/`. Transcoding memakai cwebp; alpha foreground dipertahankan dan diperiksa.
